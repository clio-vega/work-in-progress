## Part 2: the θ-fork re-derivation
- **(a) Joint cap.** Her sharpening is right. Σ_s λ_s(1+δ_s) ≤ 1 is the exact positivity condition, because the strata are independent and their minima −(1+δ_s) can occur together. Σ λ_s ≤ 1/b is a sufficient relaxation of it. But the displayed formula (reply tex l.155–159) joins the two with "**equivalently**". That is false, and her own next paragraph says so. Replace it with "implied by". Caveat on "b is F₀-measurable": this holds only for the a-priori δ_s. `stratum_epsilon` still computes ε from the simulator's true p (my 954 caveat), and the reply should not imply otherwise.
- **(c) Range → slack.** Confirmed. δ_k = 2ε_k² ≤ L² L_range²/(2Q²) is correct, and it matches my 11-27 reply.
- **(b) The θ-neighbour run.**
  - **Reproduced exactly.** With her code unchanged: null 6/500 = 0.012 and alt γ = 0.10 491/500 = 0.982 (`rerun_neighbour_R500.out`).
  - **Type-I.** 0.012 is 6/500 at nominal α = 0.05 (Ville threshold 20), R = 500. The Clopper–Pearson 95% interval is [0.0044, 0.0259], which is inside the nominal level and below it. The letter's "including a null-DGP Type-I check" is not a second check: the Type-I column *is* the `null_dgp` run.
  - **The Q-sweep is one experiment, not five.** In `theta_neighbour` mode Q enters only the midpoint label, which is never used. The rng stream is the same for every Q. The Q = 5 and Q = 80 wealth paths are **identical on 500/500 seeds**. "Flat at 0.982 across every Q" is therefore a tautology, and "Type-I 0.012 at every Q" is one measurement. That is fine, but the letter should say "Q-invariant by construction, hence one run", not present it as five confirmations.
  - **Finding: the per-item validity argument is false. The run is rescued only at the pair or block level.** The docstring of `make_theta_neighbour_block_pairing` argues E[U−V | F, θ] = p_i(s)(p_j(s)−p_j(t(s))) ≤ p_i·2ε_pair ≤ δ_pair. The last step needs p_i ≤ ε_pair, which is false. Exact enumeration (`pair_exact.out`) at her DGP's typical neighbour gap g = 1/21 gives:
    - **E[X_a] = −7.1e-3 and E[X_b] = +7.1e-3**, where δ = 2.8e-4. The harder partner's factor has conditional mean > 1, about 25δ.
    - Over random marginals in [0,1]⁴, the per-item mean reaches +0.49.
    - The **pair sum** has mean ≤ 0. In her DGP it is exactly 0, because both judges have slope ½, so ΔpᵢΔq = 2δ: the n = 2 Prop-1 bound is tight. Over the random scan its maximum is −1.5e-6.
    - The **pair product** E[(1+λX_a)(1+λX_b)] is ≤ 1: −3.3e-2 at λ = 0.9 in her DGP, and at most −1.3e-4 over the random scan. The cross moment is negative, because the two factors share W_j(a) and W_j(b) with opposite signs.

    So the wealth is a supermartingale **at block ends**, given that the pairs are independent under H0 (they are in `null_dgp`), but **not at every item**. Her `ville_reject` scans every item index. In the rerun, null rejections are **0.012 scanning every item vs 0.004 (2/500, CP [0.0005, 0.0144]) at block ends only**, so 4 of the 6 null crossings are at unlicensed mid-block times. Power drops only to 0.976. It is not a Type-I violation here, but it is a gap in the argument.
  - **Slack is oracle, but it coincides with (A_L) here.** ε_pair is computed from the true p_i, p_j. In this DGP the p's are linear in θ with slope ½ and never clipped (p_i ∈ [0.05, 0.55], p_j ∈ [0.10, 0.60]). So the oracle value equals the implementable a-priori bound L|θ_a−θ_b|/2 with L = ½, and the numbers are unaffected. The code should still compute it from θ.

## Her question: product or additive per-stratum combiner?
My answer is that **the midpoint-stratum label is not load-bearing for validity under either combiner.** What is load-bearing is (i) the unit of the factor and (ii) whether the pairs in a block are conditionally independent under H0.
- **Product** (compounding 10 bets per block). This is valid iff every factor has conditional mean ≤ 1 *given the earlier factors in the product*. Per-item factors fail that test (above). Per-pair factors 1 + λ(X_a+X_b) with λ ≤ 1/(2(1+δ_p)) pass it by the n = 2 Prop-1 pair bound alone, with no cross moment needed. The product across the pairs of a block then needs those pairs to be independent given F_{m−1} and θ. That is an assumption about the null ("items fail independently given θ"), not something the label provides. Alternatively, keep the per-item factors and claim validity only at block ends.
- **Additive** e_m = 1 + Σ_p λ_p (X_a+X_b)_p. Validity needs only E[pair sum | F_{m−1}] ≤ 0 and the joint cap Σ_p 2λ_p(1+δ_p) ≤ 1. It needs no independence between pairs, so it survives a null with within-block dependence (for example a block-level shock under H0). It pays for that by placing one bet per block instead of ten.
- **The label** enters only if λ is *tied* per stratum (λ_s shared by the pairs whose midpoint falls in s). That is a parameter-sharing device for learning λ, so it affects power, not validity.
- **Recommendation.** Use the product of per-pair factors, monitored at pair (or block) completion, and state cross-pair conditional independence explicitly as part of H0. Keep the additive version as the robustness arm for nulls that allow within-block dependence. So the answer to her fork is: neither as stated. Change the factor unit from item to pair; then the label is cosmetic in both.
