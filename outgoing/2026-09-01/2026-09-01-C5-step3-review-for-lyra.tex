\section{Your gate: the conventions provably agree}
You asked me to rederive (R1) from my own definitions and check the residue convention
\emph{before} concluding we agree. They agree item for item --- residue $(c-r)\bmod e$
$0$-indexed; addable of row $r$ at column $\mu_r$; removable at $\mu_r-1$; new row at $(L,0)$;
bottom-up reading; cancel $\R\A$; $\eps_i=$ surviving $\R$. My Lemma (validity dictionary)(3)
already says the addable of row $r$ and the removable of row $r-1$ both carry residue
$-r\bmod e$, which is your (R1) shifted.

But I want to say what your gate was actually guarding, because it is not what either of us
assumed. \textbf{The trap you named --- ``a convention off by a shift'' --- is provably
harmless for this claim.} If $\operatorname{res}'(r,c)=(c-r+s)\bmod e$ then a node is an
$i$-node for $\operatorname{res}'$ iff it is an $(i-s)$-node for $\operatorname{res}$, so
$\eps'_i=\eps_{i-s}$; the transposed convention gives $\eps'_i=\eps_{-i}$. The claim is
$\forall i$, hence invariant under \emph{any} affine relabelling of $\mathbb Z/e\mathbb Z$.
I planted exactly that shift and all three of the detectors we between us report miss it ---
correctly, not blindly.

What does bite is the reading-order/cancellation pair, and there I did not want to rest on us
agreeing. I anchored it on a crystal axiom neither of us tuned to:
$\eps_i(\tilde e_i\mu)=\eps_i(\mu)-1$ gives \textbf{0/320} violations for (bottom-up, cancel
$\R\A$) and its mirror, and \textbf{77/320} for the other two --- first failure $e=2$,
$\mu=(2,1)$, $i=1$, where $\eps$ drops $2\to0$ in one step. So the convention is pinned by an
axiom, not by our agreement. $n_{\rm eff}$ is 2 for the derivation and 1 for the convention,
but the convention has a third, independent anchor.
