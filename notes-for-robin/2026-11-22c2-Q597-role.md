# Q597 (Role): the role is a type, and the readout follows the block swap — PROVE 2026-11-22 c2

Robin — the short version. Full tex: `https://github.com/clio-vega/proofs/blob/main/2026-11-22c2-Q597-role.tex` (9 pp; PDF beside it).

**What (Role) was.** This morning's PROVE reduced G1a to (Role): in a diverging B/C pair, the journey on the side of the readout set I is the one whose first contact lands on the other journey's shared sheet. That is a statement linking the LR readout to geometry. Nothing local could prove it.

**What I found.**
1. **I belongs to the pair, not to an order.** The boards commute, so X^y − X = Y^x − Y = P12 − P1 − P2 + P. I is the support of that mixed second difference. This is a two-line proof. The brief had suspected that I depends on the order of application, and it does not.
2. **The role is a type.** In every diverging pair, exactly one of the two journeys is at a class-1 a-step when it first meets the other's wake. Call it L. This follows from the local contact classification once the handshake is mutual, i.e. once the two contact hexagons share a tile. That sharing ("Share") is computed (always exactly one M0 square, 2536 pairs) but not proved. Lemma phi's g reads L: Δg ∈ {0, −1}. ψ reads nothing.
3. **I's side is decided by L's re-run.** If L satisfies the block swap BS, then the 11-18 readout formula gives:
   - L's re-run takes b ⇒ I is nonempty and lies on L's side;
   - L's re-run takes the other a-step ⇒ I = ∅.

   The nonvanishing in the b case is proved: a first-moment argument on the twelve good configurations shows that every step of the middle pushes one entry of Σ key ⊗ Δ the same way. So (Role) ⇐ (Share) ∧ BS(L) ∧ (Ca), where (Ca) is a shape fact about the empty-I pairs. All of these are computed at size 5 and on the size-6 subsample. BS(L) holds on every diverging pair, *including* the empty-I ones. The other journey never has BS on a one-sided pair.
4. **So (Role) should not be an input at all.** For any role with contact type (1,a_T)/(1,b), (CC), (A1) and (Sheet) hold with no hypothesis on I. I re-read the G1a proofs, and none of them uses I. If the program G1 → WS → CT → BS is run on L instead of on "the I-side journey", (Role) comes out at the end as a corollary of BS(L).
5. **(MidEnd) circularity located.** A suffix version of Lemma dinv is proved by the same determinism lever: a second present configuration would stick the engine. With it, (MidEnd)'s rejoin half reduces to (Sh3), which is proved, plus (Resync), plus two small hypotheses. (Resync) is exactly BS's rejoin clause, so the circle is real and sits only there.

6. **The (MidEnd) circle is not needed for WS.** WS-b and the G1c corollary use (A2) only for (A)-tiles that are *wake* tiles. For those, (Sh3) already says where the reading step is. So (A2), hence (G1a-A), hence G1c, hold for every geometric record, with no (MidEnd), BS or I.

7. **Kind-(A) branch of WS-b, done for geometric records.** The prefix never removes u−α.
   - For tiles read at k ≥ p+2: Thm AW applied to the clean journey itself.
   - For the one tile read at p+1: the clean journey's (1,b) step there is exactly the re-run's step p, translated by α.

   With the G1c corollary, this is (A*) of Prop wsb. Then all of WS-b, in Thm wsb. For geometric records, WS-b holds given two computed statements about J_o near the shadow:
   - (A3): A-shaped read tiles have u−α ∈ M0;
   - (Tn): J_o's step after its turn b-step is not its straight step.

   Tiles read at p+1 are handled by translation onto the re-run's hexagon. The shadow steps are handled by a finite table: every tile J_o's class-0 configurations remove is B-local, A-shaped, or the turn square. WS-a holds on the same range (Prop wsa). A prefix-made tile cannot be read there, because the prefix region is made of M0 tiles that J_o never removes.
8. **The circle unwinds.** Define the candidate middle from the shadow (steps p..p+m) instead of from the re-run. Then:
   - WS on that range gives CT1;
   - WS_last gives CT2, and resync is automatic;
   - Lemma suffix shows the candidate middle is the true middle.

   So (MidEnd) is an output. Still needed: (A3), (Tn), WS_last, and (Comp). The termination pieces, (T) and the CT clause, are proved: J_o never touches the only nest A can stop in. (Comp) is the statement that the stale tiles at the rejoin are of two kinds; both kinds are proved never read again, which gives (Stale). All are computed, and none is circular.

9. **WS_last, probed.** The contact hexagon translated by γ consists of tiles J_o creates at its turn (and at the step after). So WS_last is local to the turn, apart from one strip statement along the shadow that I did not prove.

10. **Case a (empty I) is a forced square.** When L's re-run takes the other a-step, a finite placement forces both journeys' next steps. They are exact translates of each other's first step, so the square closes. (Ca) then needs only two small computed hypotheses.

**Refuted on the way.** "The later contact is the clean one" matched 521/521 at size 5. It fails at size 6, where p_x = p_y on a one-sided pair.

**Open.** (A3), (Tn), WS_last, (Stale), (T). (Share) by hand. The two-step swap (case a, the empty-I pairs) as its own BS statement. I-freeness of the later stages (G1b, G1c, WS, CT), which I have not re-read. (Resync).
