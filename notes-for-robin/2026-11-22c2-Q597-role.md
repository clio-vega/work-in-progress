# Q597 (Role): the role is a type, and the readout follows the block swap — PROVE 2026-11-22 c2

Robin — the short version. Full tex: `https://github.com/clio-vega/proofs/blob/main/2026-11-22c2-Q597-role.tex` (9 pp; PDF beside it).

**What (Role) was.** This morning's PROVE reduced G1a to (Role): in a diverging B/C pair, the journey on the side of the readout set I is the one whose first contact lands on the other journey's shared sheet. That is a statement linking the LR readout to geometry. Nothing local could prove it.

**What I found.**
1. **I belongs to the pair, not to an order.** The boards commute, so X^y − X = Y^x − Y = P12 − P1 − P2 + P. I is the support of that mixed second difference. This is a two-line proof. The brief had suspected that I depends on the order of application, and it does not.
2. **The role is a type.** In every diverging pair, exactly one of the two journeys is at a class-1 a-step when it first meets the other's wake. Call it L. This follows from the local contact classification once the handshake is mutual, i.e. once the two contact hexagons share a tile. That sharing ("Share") is computed (always exactly one M0 square, 2536 pairs) but not proved. Lemma phi's g reads L: Δg ∈ {0, −1}. ψ reads nothing.
3. **I's side is decided by L's re-run.** If L satisfies the block swap BS, then the 11-18 readout formula gives:
   - L's re-run takes b ⇒ I is nonempty and lies on L's side;
   - L's re-run takes the other a-step ⇒ I = ∅.

   So (Role) ⇐ (Share) ∧ BS(L) ∧ two small shape facts. All of these are computed at size 5 and on the size-6 subsample. BS(L) holds on every diverging pair, *including* the empty-I ones. The other journey never has BS on a one-sided pair.
4. **So (Role) should not be an input at all.** For any role with contact type (1,a_T)/(1,b), (CC), (A1) and (Sheet) hold with no hypothesis on I. I re-read the G1a proofs, and none of them uses I. If the program G1 → WS → CT → BS is run on L instead of on "the I-side journey", (Role) comes out at the end as a corollary of BS(L).
5. **(MidEnd) circularity located.** A suffix version of Lemma dinv is proved by the same determinism lever: a second present configuration would stick the engine. With it, (MidEnd)'s rejoin half reduces to (Sh3), which is proved, plus (Resync), plus two small hypotheses. (Resync) is exactly BS's rejoin clause, so the circle is real and sits only there.

**Refuted on the way.** "The later contact is the clean one" matched 521/521 at size 5. It fails at size 6, where p_x = p_y on a one-sided pair.

**Open.** (Share) by hand. The two-step swap (case a, the empty-I pairs) as its own BS statement. I-freeness of the later stages (G1b, G1c, WS, CT), which I have not re-read. (Resync).
