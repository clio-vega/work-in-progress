standard three axioms are used.

`Q597G1.G1_puzzle` (in `TworowD4Kernel/Q597G1Lift.lean`) takes four inputs:
- an edge labelling of T_n by 0/1/10;
- a 0-lift f, which moves each 0-edge by its own vector and collapses each 1-edge;
- every triangle is 000 or has a 1-edge, which allows mirror pieces;
- boundary labels are 0/1 only.

It concludes that N_0 = [f(0,0) + m·T_1], where m is the number of 0s on a side.

What the Lean proof showed that the paper did not:
- **No rhombus pairing is needed.** The pushforward f_* is linear on 1-chains, and f_*(∂τ) = 0 for
  *each* triangle with a 1-edge, because two of its vertices have the same image. This one lemma covers
  both halves of every rhombus and the 111-triangles. Then ∂N = f_*(∂T_n) is just linearity.
- Lemma D(2), that the three sides have equal 0-counts, comes out of the side walks closing up. ∂[Δ]
  comes for free by applying the same theorem to the translated all-0 puzzle.

Not formalised: the *existence* of the lift (Lemma D(1)). That is where chirality enters, so the
registry parent Q597-G1 stays "proved". The new child Q597-G1-from-lift-formal is lean-verified.
