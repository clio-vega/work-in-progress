# For Robin — Q597 (Share₂) proved; the corridor half of (Merge) proved (PROVE 2026-11-26 c2)

PDF: https://github.com/clio-vega/proofs/blob/main/2026-11-26c2-Q597-Share2.pdf (commit 54d0644)

**What is proved** (under the standing hypotheses; the extra hypothesis (H1) was proved later in the session):
- **(Share₂), the second handshake.** E₁ and L₁ remove the same two M0 tiles (a square and a triangle) outside both contact
  hexagons. Exactly one of them is a b-step. In case b they create a common class-0 rhombus R*.
- **The corridor.** From R*, E and L take *identical* class-0 a-steps until both take a (0,b) step that removes the same
  tiles. There the two targets rotate differently and the journeys part.

**How.** I did not use the monotone cycle the brief proposed. The killing constraint is that M0 is a tiling. A new,
easy lemma (finality, from MU+REM+CRE) says a journey never removes a non-rhombus tile it created. So every tile
removed at these steps is an M0 tile. A finite read of the 12-configuration table then leaves 8 of 36 joint shapes, and
(Share₂) holds on all 8. For the corridor: at a class-0 rhombus, B and C remove the same tiles for each type, and the
(S4) stopping test is target-blind. If the types differed, two overlapping M0 tiles would be needed (Lemma ovl).
c1's open "target-independence of the engine's choice" turns out to be unnecessary.

**Honest negatives.**
- (Share₂) holds on 440/440 B/B pairs too. My prediction that it would fail there was wrong. It fixes shapes and does
  not separate commuting from non-commuting pairs. The clause that does separate them is "no shared step after the
  exit": it holds 962/962 for B/C and fails 249/249 for B/B.
- DREAM's de Bruijn-zone falsifier for the corridor came back negative.

**Case b is unconditional.** A proved fact from 11-25, (F3), gives (H1) in case b and, with one finite lemma, the corridor
identity a second time, plus k = m_L − 3. So the corridor half of (Merge), which Lemma red uses, is proved in case b.
**Later in the session: (H1) is proved in every case**, so (Share₂) needs only the standing hypotheses. E's re-run front
E^L₀, E^L₁ is forced by presence plus Lemma U, and the stop-grid functionals rule out early stops. The whole front window
(W_F) is proved, tile for tile equal to the census classes.

**Still open in (E-struct).** The corridor on the *re-run* boards (Merge′). Its base case is now proved.
The back window (W_B), and (LEN) for E.

**Addendum (same session, later): (Merge′) is proved.** On the corridor, E's re-run is E's corridor translated by −α,
and it equals L's re-run, shifted one step. The proof is an induction; each step is a finite check in "absence form":
every wrong move at R_i − α would remove a tile that overlaps a tile L_i created, and the board is a tiling.
