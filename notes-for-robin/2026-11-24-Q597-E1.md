Robin, the brief asked whether (E1) (J_o's step after the split is not its straight step) is the same statement as (Tn) in Thm wsb, and then for a proof.

**Same statement.** The journey, the step e+1 and the forbidden type all agree. The index agreement is by the shadow, not by both being 0-exception statements; data 521/521 and 506/506.

**The suggested certificate does not exist.** No type of A's next hexagon at R#, none of A's created tiles, and neither nest neighbour of R# ever meets any configuration of J_o at R^o_{e+1}. On the census, the straight configuration meets no tile that A creates anywhere in its journey. The stop grid (73/521) and word factors (every 3-factor occurs) are dead too.

**What does block it** is one triangle Z1 of M0 at the split vertex. It is present at all 521 + 506 splits and absent at all 1346 + 1445 generic (0,b)→straight steps. Z1 overlaps the straight square and also the nest rhombus that a stop would need. That gives Thm E1Z: (Z1) ⇒ (E1).

**The key step:** Z1 = τ3 − α, where τ3 is the unique A-shaped triangle of the split hexagon, read by A at p+m. So (Z1) is exactly the turn instance of (A3). Theorem AW (the A-shaped branch of Thm wsb, at j = e) says J_o never removes Z1. A stop on the nest's boundary row falls to a computed frame-boundary fact (FB): the cells just outside nest B's and nest C's frames lie outside the region (1091 states). Hence:
- (E1) = (Tn) ⇐ (A3) ∧ (FB);
- WS-b for geometric records ⇐ (A3) ∧ (FB);
- (Comp)(i) ⇐ (A3) ∧ (FB).

**What is left** in Remark order: (A3), (FB), the rest of WS_last, and (Comp)(ii).

(A3) is a rigidity statement about M0 tiles that neither journey ever touches; each shadow step uncovers new ones. In the height picture, the Λ1-triangles project bijectively onto a full triangle Δ (computed), and M0 is a field of hexagons over the vertices of Δ, glued along square stacks (computed fiber lemma). (A3) then says one such hexagon has two adjacent sides of length 0. ## Update, later the same session (proofs commit 12f90d5)
(A3) now has a proof by induction along the shadow band, conditional only on structural facts about the mosaic M0.
Project to the lattice Δ in which the relevant triangles project bijectively, and record at each vertex the six
square-stack sizes. At each band vertex two consecutive stacks are empty. One is certified by J_o's previous hexagon
(a single flat pair of triangles per step type). The other is the edge it shares with the previous band vertex, or,
at the first band vertex, a flat pair in A's contact hexagon. Closure of the height function then forces the hexagon
over the vertex to degenerate onto one axis. Every A-shaped wake tile that A reads lies in the shadow (Prop sh3).
Each one either sits inside one height group or pairs the two axis stacks, so u − α ∈ M0. Net:
**WS-b, (E1) = (Tn) and (Comp)(i) ⇐ (H1)–(H4), (Cl), (FB)**: unique lifts, the jump rule across square stacks,
no rhombus over the band, the chain form of the axis stacks, closure, and the nest frames on the region boundary.
All are computed with 0 exceptions at sizes 5 and 6. Lesion: without A's contact hexagon the induction certifies 0
records, which is where the split enters. The PDF is in the proofs repo: `2026-11-24-Q597-E1.pdf`, §§ band, A3full.
