# Q597: (Share) is proved. The two contact hexagons have the same square (PROVE 2026-11-23)

Robin, the smallest open hypothesis upstream of (Role) is now closed. The proof is short, and I think it is the right shape.

**Statement.** Take two terminating journeys on one Purbhoo mosaic that read each other's wake, toward B/C, B/B or C/C. Their contact hexagons have the same square, which is a tile of M0. With Lemma hs this gives (Share) and the mutual handshake c0(x) = p_y, c0(y) = p_x.

**Why.** There is a rhombus potential Π ∈ Z^4 that both journeys climb.
- Its components are λ0, λ1, a Λ0-height and a Λ1-height, built from the no-repeat ψ_T.
- Π_B and Π_C translate by the same vector, so they can be compared across the two journeys.
- Each is non-decreasing along every journey toward its own target (by MU and the Δλ table). Only class-0 b-steps are stationary.

Removing a square pins Π on both sides of the step to the square, from a 12-row table (the "square sandwich"). So if J removes a square at step k and J' removes the same square at step j, then Π(R'_j) ≤ Π(R_{k+1}).

If the two contact squares differed, each would be removed by the other journey strictly after that journey's contact. Then Π goes once around a loop:
Π(R^y_{p_y+1}) ≤ Π(R^y_j) ≤ Π(R^x_{p_x+1}) ≤ Π(R^x_{j'}) ≤ Π(R^y_{p_y+1}).
Every step is then an equality, and the equality case forces a class-0 b-step from a class-1 rhombus. That is a contradiction.

**What it does not use.**
- B ≠ C.
- Lemma U, the choice rule, or the 11-22 local classification.
- The square lies in the wake for a one-line reason: it overlaps a tile of the re-run's (different) configuration, from a 24-row table, and that tile sits on the re-run's tiling.

The brief's suggested lever was determinism, and the lesion says it is not this one: dropping uniqueness from the local classification changes nothing. The lever is monotonicity.

**What it gives.** Prop orient (exactly one class-1 a-step role per diverging pair) is now unconditional. Thm main reads (Role) ⇐ BS(L) ∧ (Ca).

**Finite inputs.** Three reads of the twelve good configurations: the sandwich, the class of σ2-removing steps, and the square overlap. MU and the Δλ table are as before. The census corroborates every lemma (size 5 full, size-6 subsample), with live lesions: the triangle sandwich fails 3142 times, and the wrong-target Π fails 26k times.
