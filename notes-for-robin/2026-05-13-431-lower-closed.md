## Headline

The afternoon-2 dim2-331 paper said adjacent-injectivity gives within-class linear independence in each $\Phi_X$-class separately, but its Remark 4.10 concluded that this is **not** sufficient to close the full $5$-vector lower bound for $(4, 3, 1^q)$, citing possible cross-class $c_3$-overlap invisible to either position-of-$n$ projection.

**Remark 4.10 was wrong.** The reason: the $\Phi_A^\lambda$- and $\Phi_B^\lambda$-images have **disjoint seminormal SYT-supports at the $V_\lambda$ level**, BEFORE the outer chain $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$ acts. The cell pairs $\{c_1, c_3\}$ vs $\{c_2, c_3\}$ differ as unordered sets, so no SYT of $\lambda$ can be in both supports. Hence
\[
   \Phi_A^\lambda(V_{\mu_A}) \cap \Phi_B^\lambda(V_{\mu_B}) \;=\; \{0\} \quad \text{in } V_\lambda
\]
without projection. Both images lie in $E_{n-1}^+|_{V_\lambda}$. Adjacent-injectivity (universal $H_q(S_n)$-module fact) makes $\mathcal{O}_\lambda$ injective on $E_{n-1}^+|_{V_\lambda}$, so the direct sum of dim $2 + 3 = 5$ maps to dim $5$ in $B^{(\lambda)}$. QED.

## Why the previous attempt got stuck

The afternoon partial paper tried to lift position-of-$(n-2)$ separators through three layers of recursion. Position-of-letter contamination at depth 3 broke this. The fix was to give up on position-of-letter and reach for tracer-pair Hoefsmit -- a $5 \times 5$ explicit computation.

Disjoint pairs $\Rightarrow$ disjoint SYT supports $\Rightarrow$ direct sum of lifts $\Rightarrow$ adjacent-injectivity transports dim through outer chain.

The afternoon-2 author noticed within-class linear independence via adjacent-injectivity but stopped one step short. The missing step: A-vs-B decoupling is **also** an adjacent-injectivity / disjoint-supports argument at the inner level, not a separate position-of-$n$ projection argument at the outer level.

## The meta-theorem (r=2 dim-additivity)

The same proof gives a clean meta-theorem (Theorem 5.2 of the paper):

**Theorem.** Let $\lambda$ be a rank-zero shape with two length-pres corners $c_1, c_2$ and column-$1$ bottom $c_*$. Assume SRD-style reduction
\[
   B^{(\lambda)} = \mathcal{O}_\lambda[\Phi_A^\lambda(B^{(\mu_A)}) + \Phi_B^\lambda(B^{(\mu_B)})]
\]
and non-degenerate corner-pair $T_{n-1}$-blocks. Then
\[
   \dim B^{(\lambda)} = \dim B^{(\mu_A)} + \dim B^{(\mu_B)}.
\]

The proof is shape-agnostic. Combined with the dim2-331 r=1 dim-equality theorem ($\dim B^{(\lambda)} = \dim B^{(\mu_1)}$), this gives complete recursive computation of $\dim B$ for any rank-zero shape with SRD-style reduction, in the stable regime.

## Connection to the closed-form SYT theorem

The May-13 closed-form SYT paper conjectured $\dim B^{(\lambda)} = f^{\lambda^{(\ge 2)}}$ in the stable regime. The r=2 additivity is the inductive step that matches the SYT branching recursion. **A full structural proof of the closed-form SYT theorem is now within reach**, via:

1. Base case: $\dim B^{(\lambda)} = 1$ for hooks (May-13 hook paper).

The structural closed-form theorem looks like it falls out of these four pieces. **This is the most exciting consequence of today's prove session.** It promotes the May-13 closed-form result from "conjecture supported by 9 cases" to "theorem with structural proof modulo SRD framework completeness."
