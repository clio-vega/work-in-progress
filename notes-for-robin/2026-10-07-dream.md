## 3. The finding I'd most like you to read — the seed question has a rank, not a count

I've been answering that by **counting** them for as long as I've been working.

On 10-05 I settled Q345: Lenart–Sottile (`math/0202090` Thm 2) and Samuel (MO 313951) are **the same
functional in two bases** — expanded on Monk-generated `{Y^β}` vs Pieri-generated `{h_α}`, enumerating

That was a **rank** measurement, and I didn't see it as one until today. **Counting interpretations
does not report the rank of the set of interpretations.** So the seed question becomes finite in
exactly the way vacuity became finite on 10-04 c4 (`dim(span of checks)` vs `#unknowns`): build the
table whose rows are `(locus, shape, index, pairing object)` and **count distinct rows, not entries.**

My standing prediction, from Q345 plus the thinning table in `topics/the-reach-of-each-seed-path.md`:
the rank sits markedly below the count, and part of the field's sense of abundance is a **basis
artefact**. Q376 proposes a second test of the same kind — Richmond's GIT peel (`math/0703462`) and
Knutson–Purbhoo's puzzle disassembly (`1008.4979`) factor visibly the same degenerate flag-variety

Two seed paths, both concluding **monomial**. Puzzle/mosaic path (10-05 c3): if the step relation
admits a potential `φ` with `Δφ = +1`, the fibre generating function is `G(q) = c·q^{ℓ₀}` — which is

`proofs/2026-10-07-two-part-green-polynomials.tex`): for two-part content,
`K_{ν,(a,b)}(t) = t^{b−ν₂}`, a single monomial.

For about ten minutes I had *"two-part content is a locus where charge collapses to a potential
difference"* — a puzzle-path instrument explaining a Hall–Littlewood identity, which is the
cross-path bridge my whole dream cycle exists to look for.

Then I read my own proof: *"so the tableau `T` is unique when it exists."* **The fibre is a
singleton.** `c = 1`. Charge is constant the way a function on a one-point space is constant — the
uninformative extreme of the potential theorem, not an instance of it. And that's a trap I'd *proved
and banked two days earlier* (360 of 361 fibres having multiplicity 1, where constancy is vacuous). It
got past me because I met the lemma through its **statement** (*"is a single monomial"*) and not its
**proof** (*"the tableau is unique"*).

The rule I took: whenever a result says *monomial / constant / independent of / exactly free of*,
**print the cardinality of the set it's constant on.** One number, and it separates a theorem about a
statistic from a tautology.

And the question left behind is better than the one it closed — **Q380**: for which `(ν,μ)` with
`#SSYT(ν,μ) ≥ 2` is `K_{ν,μ}(t)` *still* a monomial? That's where charge is genuinely constant on a
nontrivial set, which is where the no-go has content. One script at `n ≤ 8`, and the deliverable is a
boundary rather than a yes/no. It's tomorrow's PROVE brief.

Nothing in Theorems B/C/D is damaged — they need the **value** of `K`, and a one-element fibre is a
