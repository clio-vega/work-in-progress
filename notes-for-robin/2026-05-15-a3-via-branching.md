# For Robin — 2026-05-15: a=3 j-formula proved via branching reduction

## TL;DR

**Closed the May-14 gap.** Phase A_2/A_3 for $\lambda = (2,2,2,1^{n-6})$
at $n \ge 9$ was only sketched in `2026-05-14-2a-1n-family.tex`. The new

gives a fully rigorous structural proof using a single
$H_q(S_{n-2})$-equivariant isomorphism.

**Theorem.** For $\lambda = (2,2,2,1^{n-6})$ at every $n \ge 9$:
$B_{n-2}^{(\lambda)} V_\lambda$ is 1-dimensional, contained in $E_1^-$,
supported exactly on the 8 SYTs of $S_3^{(n)}$.

**Corollary.** $\Pi^{S_n}|_{V_{(2,2,2,1^{n-6})}} = 0$ unconditionally for
all $n \ge 9$. **Second non-hook family** fully structurally proved.

## The key insight (the reduction)

Let $\mu = (2,2,1^{n-6}) \vdash n-2$. The branching iso
$\Phi: V_\mu|_{n-2} \xrightarrow{\sim} E_{n-1}^+|_{V_\lambda|_n}$
(constructed via $T_{n-1}$ +q-eigenvectors of the 2-blocks from
horizontal-strip removals of two cells) is $H_q(S_{n-2})$-equivariant
under the subalgebra generated by $T_1, \ldots, T_{n-3}$.

Using $\Phi$-equivariance plus the commutation
$R'_{n-2} (T_{n-2}+1) = (T_{n-3}+1)(T_{n-2}+1) S$ (where $S =
(T_{n-4}+1)\cdots(T_1+1)$ commutes with $T_{n-2}$), the staircase reduces:

$$B_{n-2}^{(\lambda)} V_\lambda = \mathbb{Q}(q) \cdot (T_{n-3}+1)(T_{n-2}+1) \Phi(u^{(\mu)})$$

where $u^{(\mu)}$ is the unique generator of the **May-13** j-formula
image $B_{n-3}^{(\mu)} V_\mu$.

The two remaining factors $(T_{n-3}+1)(T_{n-2}+1)$ admit an explicit
Hoefsmit case analysis on 8 SYTs, yielding the 8-element support
$S_3^{(n)}$.

## Why this matters

This is the **$T_2$-obstruction turned around**. In the May-14 note I
flagged that a clean inductive proof fails because $T_2$ (=
$T_{\text{boundary}}$) doesn't intertwine the row-deletion iso. The new
strategy *isolates* the obstruction into two explicit factors which can
be analyzed directly.

The pattern suggests that for **general $a$** at $n \ge 3a$, the
proof might iterate:
- $a$ applications of branching iso $\Phi^{(k)}$,
- producing $2(a-1)$ extra factors $\prod_k (T_{n-a+1-k}+1)$,
- each pair of factors performing one "L/R choice" in $\{L,R\}^a$.

This is conjectural but feels structurally right. The obstruction is
showing each intermediate image factors through a single isotypic
component (so $\Phi^{(k+1)}$ can be defined). At $a=3$ this works
because $\Phi(B_{n-3}^{(\mu)} V_\mu)$ is 1-dim, hence trivially
isotypic.

## Late-session update: **GENERAL-$a$ inductive reduction proved**

After the $a=3$ proof, I realized the same commutation argument
extends to **all $a \ge 2$**. The general theorem:
$$B_{n-a+1}^{(\lambda)} V_\lambda = \prod_{j=n-a}^{n-2}(T_j+1) \cdot \Phi_1(B_{n-a}^{(\mu_1)} V_{\mu_1})$$

Proved rigorously in `2026-05-15-general-a-inductive.tex` (6pp,
commit `642805c`, **unpushed**). Conditional only on Phase A
endpoint for general $a$ (a routine generalization of May-14) and
the support-pattern $S_a^{(n)}$ being preserved by the boundary
factor applications (a finite Hoefsmit case analysis at each $a$).

**$a=4$ verification at $n=12$:** Direct computation of $B_9^{(\lambda)}
V_\lambda$ for $\lambda = (2,2,2,2,1^4)$ matches
$(T_8+1)(T_9+1)(T_{10}+1) \Phi_1(u^{(\mu_1)})$ where $u^{(\mu_1)}$
is the May-15 generator at $\mu_1 = (2,2,2,1^4)$ on 10 letters.
Both 1-dim with support exactly $S_4^{(12)}$ — 16 SYTs from
$(5,6,7,8)$ through $(9,10,11,12)$, proportionality constant
$7683200/2801 \ne 0$.
