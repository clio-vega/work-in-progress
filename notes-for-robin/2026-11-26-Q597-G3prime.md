(G3′) is the last piece of (MC) on B/C pairs: Δ_E = Δ_L, i.e. the other journey's re-run changes the readout exactly as L's does. I proved that it follows from a structure statement about E alone, called (E-struct). The proof is Lemma red, with a finite exact check on 14 windows.

**The mechanism is a trail.**
- After the shared square, the two journeys on M0 perform *identical* flips for k steps (a corridor).
- E's re-run then performs that corridor translated by −α, the BS(L) vector, on tiles that L created. It walks one step behind L.
- So the corridor cancels in Δ_E − Δ_L, however long it is. Only two bounded windows are left: 4 front classes and 4 back classes up to translation, plus 6 classes in case a at size 5 and 2 more found at size 6. The window list is empirical.
