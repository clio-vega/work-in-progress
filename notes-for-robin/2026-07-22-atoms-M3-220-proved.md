# M^{(3)}_(2,2,0) alt-atom identity — proved

**PROVE cycle: 2026-07-22 (wake)**  
**Ship:** `~/projects/proofs/2026-07-22-atoms-positivity-M3-220.tex` (6 pp PDF)  
**Status:** proved cleanly; broader conjecture verified numerically.

## What's proved

**Theorem.** In `Q(t)[x_1, x_2, x_3]`,

```
M^{(3)}_{(2,2,0)}(x; t) = [2]_t·[3]_t · A^alt_{(2,2,0)}
                        + [2]_t²    · A^alt_{(2,0,2)}
                        + [2]_t     · A^alt_{(0,2,2)}
```

All three coefficients are products of Gaussian polynomials `[k]_t = 1+t+…+t^{k-1}` and lie
in `Z_{≥0}[t]`. The identity is proved by explicit coefficient-by-coefficient verification
on the six monomial basis elements of degree 4.

The **alt-atom operator** is `θ^alt_i(f) = (x_{i+1} - t x_i)/(x_i - x_{i+1}) · (f - s_i f)`.
It satisfies the nil-Hecke quadratic `(θ^alt_i)² + [2]_t·θ^alt_i = 0`, reduces at t=0 to the
Mason isobaric-atom operator, and — critically — does NOT satisfy the braid relation. Atoms
`A^alt_γ` are defined via a canonical bubble-sort reduced word.

## What's numerically verified but not proved

For every `(n, μ)` in {n=3, |μ|≤6} ∪ {n=4, |μ|≤6}, the classical HL polynomial
`v_μ(t)·P_μ(x; t)` expands t-positively in alt-atoms with **each coefficient a product of
Gaussians**. This is a broad, previously-not-observed pattern — cleanly Gaussian, no rogue
polynomial factors, no rescalings needed in the interior.

**Interior identity** (`μ_1 - μ_n < c`): `M^{(c)}_μ = v_μ(t)·P_μ(x; t)`. Interior atoms
positivity is an ordinary-HL statement in disguise.

**Boundary identity** (`μ_1 - μ_n = c`): needs a Gaussian denominator. For `μ=(3,1,0)`:
`M^{(3)}_{(3,1,0)} = [2]_t/[3]_t · P_{(3,1,0)}`. After multiplying by `[3]_t` the
alt-atom expansion is again a t-positive Gaussian combination.

## What retires and what stays open

**Retires:**
- The atoms-positivity route for `M^{(3)}_{(2,2,0)}` is closed — the identity is proven,
  coefficients are exact Gaussian products.
- Central obstacle of 07-22 `(†)` sprint reformulated: instead of "X=K" (killed by Q'_μ
  vs Q_μ structural mismatch), the natural home is **atom expansions via θ^alt with
  bubble-sort words**. No modified HL, no coset reduction, no Bernstein Y^λ needed for
  small cases.

**Open:**
- **Uniform Pieri induction.** Prove `e_r · A^alt_γ` expands t-positively in atoms, so
  vDEZ Cor. 4.2 iterated Pieri preserves positivity. This is a t-graded analogue of the
  Reiner–Shimozono / Assaf key-Pieri.
- **Closed formula for c_γ(t).** The coefficient depends on more than Bruhat length. Two
  γ of length 1 in the S_3 orbit of (2,1,0) get `[3]_t` and `[2]_t²` respectively —
  Gaussian products, but with different shapes. The formula likely factors through the
  specific reduced word (bubble sort), which is compatible with commutation moves but
  breaks braid moves.
- **Braid-invariant atom convention.** The bubble-sort convention is workable but
  awkward. A canonical θ operator satisfying braid AND positivity would be ideal. My
  earlier attempts with standard π^t (Ram–Ion) failed positivity — poles at t=1.
