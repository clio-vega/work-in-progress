# For Robin — Q597: the block swap holds for every diverging B/C pair, and one hidden hypothesis (PROVE 2026-11-25 c2)

Robin, three things came out of today's assembly.

1. **BS(L) is a theorem for every diverging B/C pair.** It rests on the standing hypotheses. The mosaic hypothesis is now written explicitly: the boards are lifted KT puzzles M0(ℓ). Two small new lemmas made the pieces fit.
   - If one role of a pair diverges, the other does too.
   - A journey and its re-run first differ at a step, never at an early stop.

   With these, every diverging pair is case a or case b and nothing else. The census agrees: 258 + 521 = 779 at size 5, and 494 + 738 = 1232 on size-6 chunks never run before, with 0 left over. Case a (11-23 c2) and case b (11-25) together cover everything.

2. **A hidden hypothesis in (Role).** This came out of the line-by-line I-freeness re-read that has been owed since 11-22. Theorem main of the 11-22 c2 role tex computes the readout set I "in the role L" through Lemma mixed. Lemma mixed assumes the two composite boards are equal, and that is (MC) for the pair, the goal of the whole program. It was never in Theorem main's hypothesis list. The data (all B/C boards commute) made the step look free.
   - So (Role) is proved only under (MC).
   - The BS(L) chain never uses I, so the block swap is unaffected.
   - I wrote the unconditional version: L's own difference Δ_L is 0 in case a and nonzero, one-sided, in case b.

3. **Where the climb stops.** Given PQ-RIGID, (MC) for a pair is *equivalent* to Δ_E = Δ_L, where E is the other journey. Once BS(L) is known, all the remaining content of (MC) sits in the other journey's re-run, the old gap G3 of 11-18. So the next target is not a lemma below (MC). It is (MC), with one half now in closed form.
   - The data: Δ_E = Δ_L on 779/779 pairs at size 5 and 1232/1232 at size 6.
   - The B/B control fails on every pair.

Step 0 (the monodromy loop DREAM proposed): I ran a single-rhombus version. Move x and y to B, then undo x's cell first. It closes on every B/C pair and every commuting B/B pair. It never closes on the non-commuting B/B pairs (336 open, 104 undefined). It measures cell-level commutation, though, not Purbhoo's flock-level monodromy, where the standard order fixes which rhombus moves first. So it says nothing against the jdt prediction. A flock-level loop needs labelled nests, and I did not build one.

Lesion of the mosaic hypothesis: move nest T's frame origin by one unit (−α) and the re-run would stop early in 180/253 C-records. (T♯) fails, and BS with it. No other unit shift does anything.
