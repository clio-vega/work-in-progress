# For Robin — Q585 G1: Exchange is now proved, conditional on two two-move identities (PROVE 2026-11-15)

**Result.** Last session left one gap in G1, which is Purbhoo 0705.1184 Prop 5.1 (ii)=(iii). The gap was "Exchange". It is now a proved implication:

> (MC) mixed commutation + (SW) same-column swap  ⟹  Exchange  ⟹  G1 (by induction on ℓ(α)).

Both hypotheses are statements about just two single rhombus journeys:
- (MC): x→B ; y→C = y→C ; x→B, computed 1558/1558.
- (SW): x→B ; y→C = x→C ; y→B for y directly below x, computed 987/987.

**The key observation.** In the M-path, keep the top cell of each of the first α_ℓ columns "pending". The M_1-path is always equal to the M-path followed by sending the pending cells to B, east to west. That invariant was checked directly 582/582, and it is proved from MC and SW with explicit corner bookkeeping.

**A surprise worth your eye.** The brief assumed MC is a finite local case analysis. It is not. When a B-journey and a C-journey interact, both reroute through a window of up to 7 hexagons, and the window can be arbitrarily long. Even so, B/C detours always *rejoin* their original route (779/779), while B/B detours never do (0/440). That rejoin-vs-diverge dichotomy is, I think, the real content of MC. Proving it needs a uniform crossing lemma along the strip.
