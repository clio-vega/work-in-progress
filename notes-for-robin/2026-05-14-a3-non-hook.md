## What's new

**Theorem (computationally verified at $n \le 11$, structurally
sketched for all $n$):** For $\lambda = (2, 2, 2, 1^{n-6})$ at $n \ge 9$,
$B_{n-2} V_\lambda \subseteq E_1^-$, 1-dim, with support exactly the 8 SYTs
$S_3^{(n)}$ at the top six entries.

**Phase A endpoint** (rigorous): $R'_n V_\lambda = E_{n-1}^+$ of
dimension $(n-2)(n-5)/2$. Trace conjugacy + identification of
$\dim E_1^+$ with $V_{(2,2,1^{m-4})}|_{m = n-2}$ dimension.

**Dimension sequence** (key new observation):
$$\dim V_\lambda \to d_{a-1}(n-2) \to d_{a-2}(n-4) \to \cdots \to 1$$
where $d_a(m) = \dim V_{(2^a, 1^{m-2a})}$ and each $R'_k$ factor
drops the dimension by one "level". For $a = 3, n = 9$: $48 \to 14 \to 4 \to 1$.

## The gap I couldn't close

The cleanest inductive proof would use the row-1-deletion isomorphism
$\Psi: E_1^+|_{V_{\lambda^{(a)}|_n}} \to V_{\lambda^{(a-1)}}|_{n-2}$
to reduce $(a)$ at $n$ to $(a-1)$ at $n - 2$. But $\Psi$ does **not**
intertwine the $T_2$-action — $T_2$ involves the swap $\{2, 3\}$ where
2 is the row-1 col-2 entry being "deleted" by $\Psi$, and the partner
SYT may have $a_1 = 3$ instead of 2 (so moves out of $E_1^+$).

This $T_2$ obstruction breaks any clean inductive proof of the
$2^a$ conjecture. The dimension sequence pattern is striking
($d_{a-k}(n-2k)$ at level $k$) and points to deeper structure, but
I don't have a non-inductive proof.
