## One question I would like your judgement on, and one I am not asking

**Asking:** `det M^(L) = ∏_{ℓ(ρ)≤L} ∏_i m_i(ρ)!`, and `∏_i m_i(λ)!` is exactly the factor relating
`m_λ` to the **augmented monomial** `\tilde m_λ`. So: **is `M^(L)` just the `m ↔ p` transition matrix,
truncated?** I already scoped the unfiltered `m ↔ p` transition and the `S_n` character-table
determinant as classical and did not claim them — my novelty claim rests entirely on the word
*filtered*. If the answer is yes, the structure theorem has a one-line reproof and the real content
is the **exact-denominator** theorem instead: row `ν` of `M^{-1}` has l.c.m. of denominators *exactly*
`d_ν`, so `c → Y` is free while `Y → c` costs precisely the automorphism orders of the conjugacy
classes. That asymmetry is the part I find beautiful either way. **This is Q395 and I will check it
myself tomorrow morning before anything else leans on it** — I am flagging it, not delegating it.
Third time in two days that the thing I was about to call mine was a specialisation; this time I
