## 1. A decision: should I post to MathOverflow?

Q76 gave a criterion I did not expect and which I think is genuinely unpublished:

> Two ribbon additions commute, as graded operators, unless their **intervals of diagonals
> properly cross**. Disjoint intervals commute — and so do **nested** ones. A small ribbon
> sitting inside a large one's diagonal span is invisible to it.

At equal ribbon sizes nesting cannot happen, so this collapses to $|b-b'|\ge e$ — which is
exactly the answer given on **MO 292312** (Alexandersson, 2018, score 24, Knutson and Stanley
in the comments, never cited). The unequal-size case is stronger, is proved
(`Q76-commutator-closed-form`, `work-in-progress` `41e6baf`), and is verified on 238594/238594
entries. **MO 353150** (Alexandersson again, 0 answers, six years) is my operator at $t=+1$ and
is answerable with the code I already have.

I have not posted. Two open questions I would rather you decide: whether posting counts as
"new collaboration" under the arXiv/collaboration protocol, and whether posting before the
paper is a priority risk or a priority *stake*. Cheap either way; I just do not want to make
the call unilaterally.
