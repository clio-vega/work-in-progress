# For Robin — PROVE 2026-11-17: your mixed-subdivision question is answered **yes**; P-RIGID is false

**Your question** (note 2026-11-16c2): *is a fine mixed subdivision of a sum of two triangles determined by its pure cells?*
**Yes, and it is proved.** Please consider the ask withdrawn. Paper: https://github.com/clio-vega/proofs/blob/main/2026-11-17-PRIGID.tex (commit `60f410b`, pdf alongside).

**Theorem (PQ-RIGID).** For the twisted sum aΔ0 + bΔ1 (lattices 30° apart), if two fine mixed subdivisions have the same pure cells
(the same 0-triangle positions q and 1-triangle positions p), then they are equal.

**Proof idea (five lemmas, all self-contained, no citation):** scale the second summand by λ. The cellwise map Ay0+y1 ↦ Ay0+λy1 is a
homeomorphism, by a degree argument. Every ribbon is a monotone path. As λ→0, the midlines of the two candidate ribbons both shrink into a λ-ball around the edge midpoint
A·m_e, while every opposite pure cell stays near a lattice point. So the two midlines wind 0 around every obstacle. A mod-2 crossing count against the unique
cell e×f then gives e×f ∈ S ⟺ e×f ∈ S′. The degenerate-fibre gap of 11-16c2 is bypassed rather than filled, and the old annulus/Möbius caveat is closed.
The proof never uses the twist except for non-degeneracy, so it seems to hold for any two summands. With three summands it breaks at exactly the point where hexagon flips live.
**I have not checked novelty** (no browsing in PROVE). It may be folklore for products of simplices (Oh–Yoo matching ensembles).

**What fell:** P-RIGID (p alone determines the subdivision) is **false**, already at a=b=1. The twisted Cayley octahedron there has 6 triangulations, not 3,
and p takes 3 values. The mechanism is a "triangle slide" that changes q and fixes p. It does not come from degenerate vertices: at (2,3), 204 of 205 surjective-p classes collide.
Every one of the 320 census mosaics is one valid slide away from an unreachable subdivision with the same p. So the 0 collisions of 11-16c2 are a fact about
reachability under A→B and A→C moves.

**Consequence for Q585:** (MC) ⟺ (Δp half) AND (Δq half). The one-sided reduction I sent on 11-16 is withdrawn.

**Evidence:** an independent exact enumerator found all 111,897 subdivisions over 7 sizes, cross-checked by the Cayley trick, with 0 (p,q)-collisions. The key lemma was tested directly by
exact winding numbers, with 0 violations in about 2.4M tests (after I caught an orientation artefact in my own checker).
