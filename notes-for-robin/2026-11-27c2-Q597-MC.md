# Q597: (MC) on B/C pairs now needs only termination. (Mos) turned out to be the converse of Lemma D, and it is proved

*Clio, PROVE 2026-11-27 c2. Proof: `proofs/2026-11-27c2-Q597-MC.tex` (proofs repo, commits 2b94a30, 66f71bd). Code: `proofs/code-1127c2-Q597/`.*

**Headline.** Take any Purbhoo mosaic with its ℤ⁴ labels and two distinct nest-A corners x→B, y→C. If the four journeys terminate, the two composite boards are equal. That is (MC) for every B/C pair. All 779 diverging pairs at size 5 were already covered computationally. This is now a proof for all sizes, with one hypothesis left: termination.

**What changed today.**
1. **The corollary.** (G3′) was proved this morning. With Prop mcE and PQ-RIGID it gives (MC) on the diverging pairs. On the non-diverging pairs, Δ_x = Δ_y = 0, so Prop mcE gives it directly.
2. **The dictionary "J_x = Purbhoo migration" survives the falsifier.** I ran it on the full 1979-tiling closure: 9742 journeys and 65802 steps, with no violation. But it holds only with our tie rule read into Purbhoo's text. 29% of steps have two candidate hexagons, which contradicts his "unique minimal hexagon" parenthesis.
3. **The dictionary turned out not to matter.** Purbhoo's Theorem 1 is about the labels of a flock, and a single rhombus has none. "M·J_x is a mosaic" follows from the engine's definition plus a finite fact: every accepted hexagon is convex with an invariant boundary, checked over all 144 local clusters. A lesion shows this vividly. If I take the *wrong* 120° rotation, 657 journeys land in nest C instead of B, yet every one of those boards is still a mosaic, and still M0(ℓ).
4. **The real content of (Mos) was the converse of Lemma D**: every ℤ⁴-mosaic is M0(ℓ) for a KT puzzle ℓ. Purbhoo §2.4 only sketches this direction (the hinge picture, credited to KT unpublished). I prove it by a degree argument for ψ = π₀+π₁. ψ never reverses orientation (a 36-case table). Nest rhombi fold onto the sides of the triangle. So the images of the triangles and squares tile T_n, and the lift is forced by Lemma D(1). The labelled-intersection half of 𝓕-membership then falls out of the edge-to-edge tiling, because φ is injective on ℤ⁴.

**What is left, precisely.**
- **Termination**, reduced to one local statement (NS), Prop term: at every step the engine selects a candidate, and that candidate is a good configuration rather than an undo. Given (NS), the no-revisit potential works on every prefix, so every journey has length at most #tiles/3. The existence half of (NS) is Purbhoo's parenthesis at l.565, which he gives without proof.
