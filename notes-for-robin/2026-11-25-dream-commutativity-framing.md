# Where Q597 sits in the literature (DREAM 2026-11-25)

Robin, this is a framing note, not a result. Nothing in it is computed, and no registry grade changed.

**Short version.** The Q597 chain, which reached BS(L) case b today, proves that two Purbhoo migrations of different type (B/C) commute. That is half of an open question Purbhoo poses at the end of his paper. The proof is direct on mosaics and does not go through jeu de taquin.

- **What KT and KTW say.** Puzzles make only 6 of the 12 symmetries manifest:
  - the rotations;
  - duality, which is mirror + 0↔1 composed with a transposition.
  - Plain commutativity is "much more non-trivial" to prove with puzzles (Knutson–Tao math/0112150 §1.1 p.4, footnote).
  - "None of these are directly visible in the Littlewood-Richardson rule" (KTW-II math/0107011 §5).
  - BROWSE 11-25 copied both quotes verbatim from the LaTeX.
- **What Purbhoo does.** Purbhoo gets commutativity by migrating flocks (arXiv:0705.1184 §1 p.2). In §5.2 he asks two questions:
  - can one see "that certain bijections were independent of choices without resorting to the fact that this is true for jeu de taquin";
  - and what the migration groupoid is, and whether it has monodromy.
  - I read both first-hand in the seed PDF.
- **Where (MC) fits.** (MC) says B/C pairs commute. It is still graded *computed*, because its reduction SCEL → BS → CT → WS is proved only in part:
  - case b of BS(L) is **proved** for geometric records;
  - case a, (Share) and an I-freeness re-read are open.
- **The other half of Purbhoo's question.** B/B pairs do *not* commute (440/779 at size 5). The open part is whether the full loop still has no monodromy. A census test for that is noted in the Q597 ledger.
