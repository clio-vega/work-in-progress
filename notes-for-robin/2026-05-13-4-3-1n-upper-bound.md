## Headline

For $\lambda = (4, 3, 1^q)$ with $q \ge 3$:
1. **Four-branch reduction:** Structurally,
   $B^{(\lambda)}_{j_0} V_\lambda = (\text{outer factors}) \cdot [\Phi_A(B^{(\mu_A)}) + \Phi_B(B^{(\mu_B)})]$
   with $\mu_A = (3, 3, 1^{q-1})$, $\mu_B = (4, 2, 1^{q-1})$, plus structurally-zero $\mu_C$-leakage and same-row-dies pieces.
2. **$j$-formula:** $B^{(\lambda)}_{j_0} V_\lambda \subseteq E_1^-|_{V_\lambda}$, **structural at $q \ge 5$**; at $q \in \{3, 4\}$ conditional on computational $E_1^-$ for $\mu_B$ at sub-shape size 8 or 9.
3. **Upper bound:** $\dim B^{(\lambda)}_{j_0} V_\lambda \le 5 = f^{(3, 2)}$
   (structural at $q \ge 5$; reduction is structural at $q \ge 3$ but the dim inputs are computational).

Matched computationally by $\dim B = 5$ at $q \in \{3, 4\}$ (mod $P = 100\,003$). Same-row-dies verified computationally for $\lambda$ at $q \in \{3, 4\}$ (rank decay $35 \to 15 \to 5 \to 1 \to 0$ at $q = 3$).

This is the **first $r = 2$ same-row family beyond $(4, 2, 1^*)$** with a structural $j$-formula at any range. It exercises the full toolkit accumulated this week.

## Important correction during the session

I initially claimed structural range $q \ge 3$. The computational sanity check at $q = 2$ flagged a real issue (rank $= 1$ not $0$), and digging in revealed: the hook $j$-formula on $\nu_2 = (4, 1^{q+1})$ requires $n_{\nu_2} \ge 2k = 8$, which forces $q \ge 3$ — fine. But the sub-shape $j$-formulas needed for the $\Phi_A, \Phi_B$ pieces (SRD-331 and SRD-421) have their *own* structural cutoffs at sub-shape size $\ge 10$, which forces $q \ge 5$. The paper was updated to be honest: reduction is structural at $q \ge 3$; full j-formula and dim bound are structural only at $q \ge 5$. **Lesson logged**: structural-input chains need to be tracked all the way down, not just the immediate ingredient.

## Proof toolkit (one paper, four ingredients)

The proof is a *clean assembly* of four prior results into a 4-branch reduction:

1. **Phase A decomposition** at $\lambda$:
   $E_{n-1}^+|_{V_\lambda} = \Phi_A(V_{\mu_A}) \oplus \Phi_B(V_{\mu_B}) \oplus \Phi_C(V_{\mu_C}) \oplus S$
   - $\mu_A = (3, 3, 1^{q-1})$, $\mu_B = (4, 2, 1^{q-1})$, $\mu_C = (3, 2, 1^q)$
   - $S$ = same-row part at row 2 (with $\nu_2 = (4, 1^{q+1})$ a hook).

2. **Three $\Phi$-pieces commute through the iteration** (operator equality in $H_q(S_n)$, index-gap-based). For each $X \in \{A, B, C\}$:
   $$P_{j_0, n-1} \Phi_X(V_{\mu_X}) = (T_{j_0-1}{+}1)\cdots(T_{n-2}{+}1) \, \Phi_X\!\left(P^{(\mu_X)}_{j_0-1, n-2} V_{\mu_X}\right)$$

3. **$\mu_C$-piece vanishes** by $j$-formula leakage: on $\mu_C = (3, 2, 1^q)$ the $j$-formula (May-12 today) gives $B^{(\mu_C)}_{j_0(\mu_C)} V_{\mu_C} \subseteq E_1^-$, and the one extra $R'_{j_0-1}^{(\mu_C)}$ step starts with $(T_1{+}1)$, which kills $E_1^-$.

4. **Same-row part vanishes** by the **SRD-421 general framework** (Theorem 14): with $\lambda_2 = 3 \ge 3$ and the hook $j$-formula on $\nu_2 = (4, 1^{q+1})$ from Shift Lemma all-hooks, the framework gives $P_{j_0, n-1} S = 0$.

After these reductions:
$$B^{(\lambda)}_{j_0} V_\lambda = (\text{outer factors}) \cdot \left[\Phi_A(B^{(\mu_A)}) + \Phi_B(B^{(\mu_B)})\right]$$
with $\dim B^{(\mu_A)} = 2$ and $\dim B^{(\mu_B)} = 3$. Total $\le 5$.

$E_1^-$ containment propagates because:
- $B^{(\mu_A)}, B^{(\mu_B)} \subseteq E_1^-$ on their respective $V_{\mu_X}$
- $\Phi_X$ is $T_1$-equivariant (index $1 \le n - 3$ for $n \ge 4$)
- Outer factor indices $\ge j_0 - 1 = q + 2 \ge 5$ commute with $T_1$

## What makes this proof clean

The four-piece Phase A decomposition is the *first time* I've combined:
- the $r = 2$ three-branch reduction (May-12 evening-3, originally for $(4, 2, 1^*)$),
- the same-row-dies framework (SRD-421 Theorem 14, established in general),
- the $j$-formula leakage trick (May-12 evening-3),
- the hook $j$-formula all-$k$ via Shift Lemma (May-13),

in a single proof. Each ingredient pulls its weight; without any one, the upper bound fails.

The **same-row-dies framework is the new lever**: it lets a single hook $j$-formula on $\nu_2$ kill the same-row obstruction $S$, even when $\dim S$ is large (35 at $q = 3$, 56 at $q = 4$).

## What's deferred

- **Structural lower bound** $\dim \ge 5$. The template is May-12-Eve3's position-of-$n$ + position-of-$(n-1)$ separators applied to 5 explicit chain vectors (2 from $\mu_A$-chains, 3 from $\mu_B$-chains). Non-trivial but not new technique. Should be a follow-up paper.
- **$q = 2$ case**. Requires $j$-formulas on $(3, 3, 1)$ at $q' = 1$ and $(4, 2, 1)$ at $q' = 1$ — neither in the SRD-331/SRD-421 ranges. Likely requires separate small-case proofs.
