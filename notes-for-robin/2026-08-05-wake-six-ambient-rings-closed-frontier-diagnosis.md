### Probe 2: Cluster parking functions (arXiv:2402.03052, Douvropoulos-Josuat-Vergès, Feb 2024, 40pp)

Verdict: **NOT THE ANCESTOR EITHER.**

The DJV23 sequel supplies the module (top homology of $\mathrm{CPF}^{(m)}(W)$) realising the $\gamma$ formula as equivariant Euler characteristic, but only for the trivial-isotypic component of $\mathrm{Ind}^W_{W_X}$. ### Probe 3: Rhoades Big-VG (arXiv:2508.18602, solo Rhoades, Aug 2025 v1 / Mar 2026 v3, 26pp)

Target-matched at dim 6 (the $\lambda = (2,2)$ block-orbit summand contribution). Ran three restriction/projection readings to outer $S_r = S_2$:

- (D2) Post-induction (Ind to $S_4$, restrict to $\langle (02)(13) \rangle$): trivial $(3q^2 + q^3)$ vs target $(1+q^2+q^4)$; sign $(2q^3)$ vs target $(q+q^2+q^3)$ — arithmetic mismatch AND degree-support mismatch.
- (D4) Full Big-VG restricted (ungraded): dim 39+36 = 75 vs target 6 — 12× too large.

**Five obstructions catalogued:**
1. Dimension (pre-induction summand is dim 2)
2. **Degree-support truncation** — the $\lambda = (2,2)$ summand is forced into window $[n-\ell, n-1] = [2,3]$; target lives in $[0,4]$. Rigid consequence of Rhoades' (5.10) filtration, no shift can fix it.

**The critical new obstruction is #5** — this is the one that distinguishes ambient-ring candidates that MIGHT work from those that CAN'T. Any candidate carrying a topological (codim-of-flat) grading will fail the same way, structurally, regardless of what else it does right.

## Frontier diagnosis (new)

Six probes, three frameworks, all NEG or NOT-ANCESTOR. This is not bad luck — it's structural.

**What all six failing candidates have in common:**
- Each carries a canonical grading rooted in geometric/topological data (codim, arrangement rank, matrix rank, cluster-complex face dimension)
- Each has a "canonical CSP polynomial" formula (either the $\mu_\lambda(q) = \prod[e_i + \cdots]/\prod[d_i]$ shape, or a rook-count-shifted analog)
- Each has a group action whose orbits index by some geometric refinement (parabolic class, block partition, rook subset)

**What Clio's $m_\pi(t)$ has that none of these produces:**
- Grading from Springer/fake-degree (palindromic, symmetric-function-side)
- Isotypic decomposition indexed by irreducibles $\pi \vdash r$ of an **outer symmetric group action on wreath-structured cosets**, not by orbits of a linear group on a variety
- Cardinality = multinomial $\binom{rk}{k,k,\ldots,k}$ = number of ordered set partitions of type $(k^r)$, not a Fuß-Catalan or cluster-complex face count

**Conjecture (new, to sit in questions/):** Module-level upgrade of Theorem D, if it exists, is a **wreath Springer construction** — a graded module for $S_{rk}$ on which the outer $S_r$ acts, whose isotypics compute $m_\pi(t)$. This lands closer to Douglass-Pfeiffer-Röhrle wreath Springer + Chmutov-Gouwens type constructions than to Rhoades-school orbit harmonics.

Alternate reading: Rigidity (2026-07-29) IS the sharpest module-level statement, and the character-level Theorem D + quartet is where the story ends for now — until someone (Clio or the community) invents the correct wreath Springer ambient.
