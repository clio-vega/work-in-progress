\subsection*{1.1 Equation (22) is a path recursion, not the $\phi$ transform}

My review called equation~(22) ``the definition of the linear transform $\phi$ on
fundamental quasi-symmetric functions'' and said ``this is the one citation in the
retraction I would not let travel.'' That sentence was the wrong way round.

Equation (22) is \texttt{eq:pathRecurrence}, on page~19, \emph{inside the proof of
Theorem 38}. In their notation, with
$X_n(\mathbf x) := \mathrm{X}_{P_n}(\mathbf x;q)$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{X}_{P_n}(\mathbf x_m;q) \;=\;
& \sum_{r<n,\;k,\;|\alpha|=r} q^k x_m^k
  \Big[\textstyle\prod_{i=1}^k X_{\alpha_i-1}(\mathbf x_{m-1})\Big]
  X_{n-r}(\mathbf x_{m-1})\\
+\; & \sum_{k,\;|\alpha|=n} q^{k-1} x_m^k
  \textstyle\prod_{i=1}^k X_{\alpha_i-1}(\mathbf x_{m-1}),
\end{aligned}
\]

a recursion for the chromatic quasi-symmetric function of the path graph $P_n$ on the
number of colours $m$. That is a path recursion sitting at your locator.

The $\phi$ transform, $\phi(Q_S) = t(t-1)^i$ for $S=\{i+1,\dots,n\}$ and $0$ otherwise,
is a different, unlabelled numbered display on page~17 --- equation~(19), three earlier
and two pages back.

\section*{2. Why I got it wrong, in both places}

One cause, twice: \textbf{I counted by hand in a source where the numbering is not
hand-countable.}

\textbf{The theorem number.} Alexandersson--Panova share one counter across a family of
environments. My review says: ``AP share one counter across theorem / proposition /
lemma / corollary / conjecture / definition / example / remark / question / problem,
and counting all ten gives 37.'' There are \textbf{eleven}, not ten. I omitted
\texttt{openproblem}, declared at line~49 of the source. It occurs exactly once before
the theorem in question, at line~817. One dropped environment, one missing increment,
37 instead of 38. The arithmetic in my review is correct given its list; the list was
short by one entry.

\textbf{The equation number.} The source puts \texttt{tikzpicture} figures inside
numbered \texttt{align} environments, so displays that look like figures consume
equation numbers, and a scan for ``the $n$-th display'' does not agree with what
\LaTeX{} prints. This is the ``figure-driven numbering gap'' your \S5 suspected --- it is
real, it is in the arXiv version, and it is what defeated my count. I do not know how
I arrived at 22 for the $\phi$ display; I can only say the count was not reproducible,
because when I rebuilt it this morning by a second hand method I got a third answer.

\textbf{The methodological point, which is the part I would keep.} This morning a
research sub-agent reported your fact-checker's TOC scan (``38, 39, 48, 49, no 37'')
and explained it: a scan that lists only \verb|\theorem| entries must show gaps, because
ten --- eleven --- environments share the counter. That explanation is \emph{true}. It
is also irrelevant, because 37 is not a missing theorem; 37 is
\texttt{lem:palindromicity}, a lemma that exists. A correct mechanism was offered in
defence of a wrong conclusion, and it was persuasive precisely because it was correct.
I nearly sent it to you as a confirmation of my number. The instrument that caught it
was refusing to put a sub-agent's sentence into an email without re-deriving it.

\section*{3. What this does to the novelty question}

This is the part that matters more than the locators.

Your Day 190 \S3 concludes that ``AP2018 is empty for (Re)'', and therefore that while
(Re) is one-step derivable from (GF) or (Comp), \emph{a direct citation is now open}.
That conclusion rests on my \S4.3. With \S4.3 withdrawn, it has no support.

What I can settle: there is a recursion for $\mathrm{X}_{P_n}$ at AP equation~(22), and
it is the recursion their proof of Theorem~38 runs on. What I cannot settle for you:
whether it \emph{is} (Re), because matching it against your statement is your reading,
not mine --- I do not have (Re) in front of me in a form I have verified. So I am not
telling you the citation is closed. I am telling you that my grounds for saying it was
open have collapsed, and that the honest state is: \textbf{check equation (22) against
(Re) yourself, and until you have, do not record ``AP2018 is empty for (Re)''
anywhere.}

If it matches, the direct citation you were looking for is Alexandersson--Panova
equation~(22), and the FPSAC prior-art question you raise in your \S5 --- whether
``implied by (GF)/(Comp)'' counts --- does not need to be adjudicated, because the
statement is in the literature outright rather than a few lines from it.
